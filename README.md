# CCM

### Strategic Intelligence for Complex Environments

CCM is a developing portfolio and applied research space focused on the intersection of **strategy, politics and communications**.

The project explores how structured analysis, data and technology can support organisations operating in volatile, uncertain, complex and ambiguous environments.

## Mission

Turn political and institutional complexity into decision-relevant intelligence, clear strategic options and effective communications.

## Featured project

### [CCM Lead Intelligence](https://github.com/Martins248/ccm-lead-intelligence)

A privacy-conscious Python toolkit for transparent lead qualification, human-reviewed outreach and Business Intelligence reporting. It includes explainable scoring, CSV safety controls, decision-oriented charts and an automated test suite.

## Core areas

- Political and institutional intelligence
- Strategic analysis and scenario development
- Political risk and contextual assessment
- Stakeholder and narrative analysis
- Strategic communications and positioning
- Decision support in VUCA environments

## Approach

CCM work is guided by four principles:

1. **Evidence** — distinguish verified information from assumptions and judgement.
2. **Context** — interpret events within their political, institutional and social environment.
3. **Anticipation** — identify signals, risks and plausible developments before they become obvious.
4. **Clarity** — translate complexity into useful choices and communication.

## Project roadmap

- Publish a transparent analytical methodology
- Develop reusable templates for strategic and political analysis
- Produce documented case studies using public information
- Explore responsible applications of data and AI to political intelligence
- Build practical tools for monitoring, assessment and decision support

## Status

**Foundation phase.** The repository currently establishes the project's scope, standards and development direction. Research, methods and tools will be added progressively.

## Ethics

All public work in this repository will rely on lawful and ethical methods, clearly distinguish analysis from fact, respect privacy, and avoid publishing confidential client information.
